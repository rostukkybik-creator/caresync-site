Refund Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

CareAlong is free to download. Any purchase in the app, such as a CareAlong Plus subscription, is made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, so refunds are handled by Apple, and we can't issue them ourselves.

How to request a refund

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
  2. Choose "Request a refund", select the reason and the CareAlong purchase, and submit.

Apple's policies apply, and depending on where you live you may have a legal right to withdraw from a purchase. In the EU and UK this is usually 14 days, which Apple handles through the same page.

Cancelling a subscription

Open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, choose CareAlong and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

Need help?

If something went wrong with a purchase, email dev1@5hz.io and we'll help however we can.
