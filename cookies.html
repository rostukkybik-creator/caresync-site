Cookie Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This website uses no cookies and no similar technologies (local storage, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or analytics). That's why there's no cookie banner: there's nothing to agree to.

What the website does use

Only what's needed to deliver the pages. Our website host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, page requested and time, for security and to keep the site running, as described in our Privacy Policy. There are no third-party embeds, fonts, videos or maps.

The app

The CareSync app doesn't use cookies. It stores your settings and care information on your device. It stores an anonymous statistics ID only if you turn on "Share anonymous usage statistics"; that is off unless you choose it.

If this changes

If we ever add anything to the website that isn't strictly necessary, such as analytics, we'll ask for your consent first and update this page.
