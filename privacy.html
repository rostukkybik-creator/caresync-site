Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

The short version: your care information stays on your device. It never goes to our servers: CareAlong uses none. Your own iCloud keeps only two small records, the care circle and your display name, and no care information. There's no account, no usage statistics, no ads, no tracking and no selling of data. This website uses no cookies.

Who we are

CareAlong is provided by Volodymyr Huz, vul. Starytskoho, Lviv, Ukraine ("we", "us"). We don't receive the information you enter in the app. We are the data controller only for the emails you send us and for this website, as described below. Contact us about anything in this policy at dev1@5hz.io.

Where your care information is kept

  • On your device, in the app's private storage. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a small summary of today's schedule on the same device, and your Apple Watch gets today's list directly from your iPhone. Documents you add are also encrypted by the app on the device.
  • In your device backups, if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, like any app's data. That's what lets you restore it on a new phone. Apple or your computer holds these backups, not us.
  • In your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit, private database): only the care circle, with the cared-for person's name in an encrypted field and their time zone, and the name you go by in the app. No medicines, notes, readings or other care information is stored in iCloud.
  • Wherever you send it. When you share a weekly summary, visit summary or emergency card through Messages, Mail, AirDrop or a printer, it goes to the people and apps you choose. We don't see it.

Who processes data for us

  • Apple (iCloud, the App Store): the two records in your private iCloud database, and app distribution. Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages, United States): hosting this website.

We don't sell or rent personal information, we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we don't use advertising, analytics, tracking or data-broker services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU, UK, Ukraine and US states such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Washington), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information. You can also object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect what was done before. For anything we hold, such as your emails, write to dev1@5hz.io. We'll answer within 30 days, or tell you within that time why we need longer, as the law where you live allows. We won't treat you differently for using your rights. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority, and in some US states you can appeal our decision by replying to it.

Because your care information stays on your device, we don't hold it and can't read, export or delete it for you. You can see, change, share and delete it yourself in the app.

Deleting your information

  • Single items: delete them in the app.
  • A whole care circle: Family › Security › Delete This Care Circle. It removes the circle and everything in it from this device and its record from your iCloud. This can't be undone.
  • Everything on a device: delete the app. Your device backups keep a copy until they are replaced or deleted.
  • CareAlong's iCloud records: in Settings › [your name] › iCloud, under the storage used by apps, choose CareAlong and delete its data.
  • Emails you sent us: ask us and we'll delete them.

Security

Your care information stays in the app's storage on your device, protected by iOS. Documents are also encrypted by the app, and you can turn on a Face ID or passcode lock. The cared-for person's name in iCloud is kept in a field Apple encrypts. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

CareAlong is for adults coordinating care and is not directed at anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Health information, US consumer health data and HIPAA

Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy describes the health information involved and your rights over it under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. CareAlong isn't a healthcare provider or health plan and isn't covered by HIPAA.

This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking and has no third-party embeds. See the Cookie Policy.

Changes

Medical disclaimer

CareAlong is a care organiser, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's and pharmacist's instructions, and call emergency services in an emergency.
