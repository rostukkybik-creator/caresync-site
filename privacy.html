Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

The short version: we can't read your family's care information. Everything you enter about the person you look after stays on your devices and is shared, end-to-end encrypted, only with the people you invite. An account is optional. Anonymous usage statistics are off unless you turn them on. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data. This website uses no cookies.

Where your care information is kept, and why we can't read it

  • On your devices, in the app's private storage. The Home Screen widget reads a small summary of today's schedule on the same device.
  • In an end-to-end encrypted store, so it reaches the people you invite. Each item is encrypted on your device, before it leaves, with a key that only your family's devices hold. The encrypted copies are kept by Google Cloud Firestore as our processor. Neither we nor Google can decrypt them. Next to each encrypted item the store keeps only an identifier, its type (for example "medicine"), when it last changed and whether it was deleted.
  • In your iCloud account (Apple CloudKit), which holds only how your family is set up: the care circle, the cared-for person's name and time zone, who belongs to it, and the encryption key, in CloudKit's end-to-end encrypted fields. No health information is stored in iCloud.

Who processes data for us

We share information only with these service providers, under contracts that limit them to providing their service to us:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check): accounts, the encrypted store and abuse protection. Google may process data in the United States. Transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Firebase privacy information.
  • Apple (iCloud, Sign in with Apple, App Attest, the App Store): family sharing, optional sign-in, app integrity checks and, if you subscribe, payments. Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • TelemetryDeck (Germany): anonymous usage statistics, only if you turn them on. TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.
  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages, United States): hosting this website.

The people you invite to a care circle can see that circle's information. That's the purpose of the app, and you control who is in it. We don't sell or rent personal information, we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we don't use advertising, tracking or data-broker services.

Anonymous usage statistics

Off unless you turn them on, during setup or in the app under Family › Privacy. If you do, the app sends events such as "a medicine was marked as taken", rough counts such as "3–4 people help", and technical details such as app and iOS version, device model, language and text size. It never sends names, medicines, doses, notes, check-in answers, appointments, contacts, phone numbers, photos or anything else you type. Statistics use a random ID made for the installation, which TelemetryDeck receives only as a one-way hash, and a new one is made if you turn them off and on. Turning them off stops sending immediately.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU, UK, Ukraine and US states such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Washington), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information. You can also object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect what was done before. Most of this you can do yourself in the app (see below). For anything else, email dev1@5hz.io. We'll answer within 30 days, or tell you within that time why we need longer, as the law where you live allows. We won't treat you differently for using your rights. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority, and in some US states you can appeal our decision by replying to it.

Because care information is end-to-end encrypted, we can't read or export it for you. You and your family hold it on your devices, and you can delete it there.

Deleting your information

  • Single items: delete them in the app.
  • A care circle: leave it, remove members or stop sharing in the Family tab. When someone is removed, the family key is replaced, so they can't read anything shared afterwards, and the encrypted copies made with the old key are deleted from our store about a month later.
  • A whole care circle, everywhere: the Primary Caregiver can use Family › Security › Delete This Care Circle. It's removed from every family member's device and from iCloud, and its encrypted copies are deleted from our store within two days.
  • Your account: Family › Account › Delete Account. This deletes your account details from our sign-in service and, for Sign in with Apple, revokes our access to your Apple ID.
  • Everything on a device: delete the app.
  • Encrypted copies in our store: deleting an item leaves a small encrypted marker so your family's other devices delete it too; our store removes these markers automatically about 90 days later. If you can't use the app, email us and we'll delete a circle's encrypted records within 30 days.
  • Usage statistics: turn them off. They are anonymous, so we can't find one person's statistics.

Security

End-to-end encryption (AES-256-GCM), records that can't be altered or deleted by our servers without the family key, app integrity checks, access rules on our database, optional Face ID / passcode lock, and no care data in device backups once the encrypted store is in use. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

CareSync is for adults coordinating care and is not directed at anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.
