Accessibility

Effective 29 October 2026

CareAlong is made for families caring for older people and people with health conditions, so accessibility is part of the design, not an extra.

The app

  • VoiceOver labels and hints on every control, and Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility sizes.
  • Care Receiver Mode: very large text and buttons, simple wording and "Read it to me".
  • Colours chosen for contrast (at least 4.5:1 for text) in light, dark and Increase Contrast modes.
  • Reduce Motion respected, and haptics alongside visual confirmation.

This website

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. The site has a skip link, a visible keyboard focus ring, semantic headings and landmarks, text contrast of at least 5:1 in light and dark mode, and works with screen readers and browser zoom.

Tell us about a barrier

If anything in CareAlong or on this site is hard to use, email dev1@5hz.io. We aim to reply within 5 working days.
