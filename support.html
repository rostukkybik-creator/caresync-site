Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email dev1@5hz.io and we'll get back to you. Please don't include medical details you wouldn't want in an email.

CareAlong is a family coordination tool, not a diagnostic medical device. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Getting started

How do I invite my family?
Open the Family tab and tap "Invite Family or a Caregiver". Send the link by Messages, Mail or any app. Each person needs an iPhone or iPad signed in to iCloud.

Everyone sees different information. What's wrong?
Make sure every device is signed in to iCloud (Settings → [your name]), is online, and that each person has accepted the invitation. Changes usually appear within seconds, but can take longer on a weak connection.

Can I use CareAlong for my own medicines?
Yes. During setup choose "I'm looking after my own health". You get large buttons and reminders written for you, and you can still invite family later.

Your account

Do I need an account?
No. An account is optional: tap "Continue without an account" during setup. You can create one later in Family › Account, with your email or with Sign in with Apple. The person you care for is never asked for one.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset link. If you added a mobile number (it's optional), you can also sign in with a code texted to it.

I didn't get the text message.
Check the number includes your country code (for example +1 or +44), wait a minute, then tap "Change Number or Resend".

How do I delete my account?
Family → Account → Delete Account. Your family's shared care information is not affected.

How do I delete a care circle and everything in it?
The Primary Caregiver can use Family → Security → Delete This Care Circle. It's removed from every family member's device, and from our servers within two days. This can't be undone.

Care Receiver Mode

What is it?
A simple screen for the person being cared for, with big buttons for "I took it", today's medicines and appointments, and one-tap calls to family. Turn it on in Family → This Device → Care Receiver Mode.

How do I switch back to the caregiver view?
At the bottom of the Care Receiver screen, press and hold "Switch to Caregiver Mode" for two seconds. The long press stops it being switched off by accident.

Reminders

I'm not getting reminders.
Check Settings → Notifications → CareAlong and make sure notifications are allowed. If the app shows a "Reminders are off" banner, tap it to fix this. Focus modes can also silence notifications.

Can I mark a medicine as taken from the notification?
Yes. Press and hold the notification and tap "I Took It" or "Mark as Done". Everyone in the family sees it.

Siri and widgets

What can I say to Siri?
"I took my medicine in CareAlong", "What's my next medicine in CareAlong" and "Tell my family I'm OK with CareAlong".

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for CareAlong.

Your data

Where is my information stored?
On your devices, and end-to-end encrypted so it reaches only the people you invite. Your iCloud holds only how your family is set up. We can't read your care information. Read the Privacy Policy for details, including how to delete everything.

What are "anonymous usage statistics"?
Counts of which features are used, such as "a medicine was marked as taken", with no names, medicines, notes or photos. They're off unless you turn them on, during setup or in Family → Privacy, and they help us decide what to improve.
