Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email dev1@5hz.io and we'll get back to you. Please don't include medical details you wouldn't want in an email.

CareAlong is a care organiser, not a diagnostic medical device. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Getting started

Do I need an account?
No. CareAlong has no accounts and no sign-in. Open the app, choose who will use it on this phone, and start adding medicines and routines.

Can I use CareAlong for my own medicines?
Yes. During setup choose "I'm looking after my own health". You get large buttons and reminders written for you.

How do I keep my family informed?
CareAlong works on one iPhone or iPad, and family don't need the app. Send them the weekly summary, or a summary after a doctor's visit, by Messages or Mail with the Share button.

How do I delete a care circle and everything in it?
Family → Security → Delete This Care Circle. It's removed from this device and from your iCloud. This can't be undone.

Care Receiver Mode

What is it?
A simple screen for the person being cared for, or for anyone looking after their own health, with big buttons for "I took it", today's medicines and appointments, "Read it to me", and one-tap calls to family. If a medicine isn't marked as taken, a gentle follow-up comes 30 minutes later. Turn it on in Family → This Device → Care Receiver Mode.

How do I switch back to the caregiver view?
At the bottom of the Care Receiver screen, press and hold "Switch to Caregiver Mode" for two seconds. The long press stops it being switched off by accident.

Reminders

I'm not getting reminders.
Check Settings → Notifications → CareAlong and make sure notifications are allowed. If the app shows a "Reminders are off" banner, tap it to fix this. Focus modes can also silence notifications.

Can I mark a medicine as taken from the notification?
Yes. Press and hold the notification and tap "I Took It" or "Mark as Done".

Siri and widgets

What can I say to Siri?
"I took my medicine in CareAlong" and "What's my next medicine in CareAlong". You'll find more in the Shortcuts app.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for CareAlong.

Your data

Where is my information stored?
On your device, and in your device backups if you use them. It never goes to our servers. Your iCloud holds only the care circle and your display name, no care information. Read the Privacy Policy for details, including how to delete everything.

I'm getting a new phone. How do I keep my information?
Your care information is kept on the device, so move it with an iCloud Backup or a computer backup, or with Quick Start when you set up the new phone. Don't delete the app before you've moved it: that erases the information on that phone.

Does CareAlong collect usage statistics?
No. There are no statistics, no ads and no tracking.
