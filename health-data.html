Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy supplements our Privacy Policy. It covers "consumer health data" as defined by US state laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's SB 370.

What consumer health data the app handles

Information you or your family enter or allow the app to read: medicines and doses, schedules and whether doses were taken, allergies, conditions, care notes, check-ins about how someone feels, doctor's appointments and questions, health journal readings (blood pressure, weight, blood sugar, pain, mood), heart rate, blood oxygen and Apple Watch heart notifications (only if the cared-for person turns on "Share watch readings"), documents you scan, and the emergency card.

Where it comes from and why it's used

It comes from you, your family members in the care circle, and, with the cared-for person's permission, Apple Health on their own phone. It's used only to show it to the people in the care circle: reminders, the shared schedule, summaries, the emergency card and heart alerts. It is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling or any other purpose.

Who can see it

Only the people in the care circle. It is end-to-end encrypted on the device before it leaves, so our processor that stores the encrypted copies (Google Cloud Firestore) and we ourselves cannot read it. We don't sell consumer health data, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Your consent and rights

You consent by choosing to enter and share information in a care circle, and for Apple Health readings by turning on "Share watch readings". You can withdraw consent by deleting the information, turning the setting off or leaving the circle. You can ask whether we hold your consumer health data, for a list of the processors involved, and for deletion. Email dev1@5hz.io. We answer within 45 days and you can appeal our answer by replying to it. If an appeal is denied, you can contact your state attorney general.
