Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This policy supplements our Privacy Policy. It covers "consumer health data" as defined by US state laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's SB 370.

What consumer health data the app handles

Information you enter: medicines and doses, schedules and whether doses were taken, allergies, conditions, care notes, check-ins about how someone feels, doctor's appointments and questions, health journal readings (blood pressure, weight, blood sugar, pain, mood), documents you scan, and the emergency card. CareAlong 1.0 doesn't read Apple Health data.

Where it is kept and why it's used

It stays on your device (and in your device backups, if you use them). It's used only to show it to you there: reminders, today's schedule, widgets, your Apple Watch, the summaries and the emergency card. It never goes to our servers, and we don't collect, receive or store it. Your private iCloud holds only the care circle (the cared-for person's name and time zone) and your display name, with no health information. It is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling or any other purpose.

Who can see it

You, on your device, and the people you choose to send a summary or emergency card to through Messages, Mail, AirDrop or a printer. We can't see it. We don't sell consumer health data, and we don't share it with anyone.

Your consent and rights

You consent by choosing to enter information in the app, and you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting it there (Family › Security › Delete This Care Circle removes everything). Because we don't hold your consumer health data, you can see and delete all of it yourself. You can still ask us whether we hold any, which processors are involved, or for deletion: email dev1@5hz.io. We answer within 45 days and you can appeal our answer by replying to it. If an appeal is denied, you can contact your state attorney general.
