Care, calmly organised.

Medicines, appointments and daily care for someone you love, or for yourself, in one calm place.

  • Everything due today, with gentle reminders and one tap to mark it done or skipped
  • Medicines with photos and dosage, and pharmacy-label scanning to add them quickly
  • Care Receiver Mode: big, simple buttons for the person being cared for, or for anyone looking after their own health, with "Read it to me" and a gentle follow-up for untaken medicine
  • Doctor's visits with your questions and a one-page summary to print or share
  • A health journal with charts, daily check-ins, and refill tasks before a medicine runs out
  • Keep family in the loop: send a weekly summary by Messages or Mail
  • Widgets, Apple Watch and Siri, in 10 languages
  • Private by design: your care information stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking

Free to download and use, with no in-app purchases. Documents, the emergency card, history and trends and the weekly summary are all included.

Coming soon to the App Store.

CareAlong is a care organiser, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it isn't an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
