Care, together.

One shared plan for the family looking after someone they love.

  • Medicines, meals and routines with gentle reminders, and one tap to mark them done
  • See who gave what, and when, so everyone knows what has already been done
  • Care Receiver Mode: big, simple buttons for the person being cared for
  • Doctor's visits with shared questions and a one-page summary to print
  • Refill warnings, daily check-ins and a weekly family summary
  • Private by design: your family's care information is end-to-end encrypted, so only the people you invite can read it

Coming soon to the App Store.

CareSync is a family coordination tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it isn't an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
